Background

A bit about
my background

With degrees in both mechanical engineering and technology, I bring a blend of theoretical knowledge and hands-on manufacturing insight to the table. I am highly self-driven, a trait that led me to self-teach modern CAD platforms, represent my school in a competition, and take on freelance commissions.

Outside of formal projects, I find myself tinkering with different software — exploring various features just to see what they're capable of. Along the way, I've also picked up a few creative tools, like the Adobe Suite, which I use to edit and polish some of my other work.
